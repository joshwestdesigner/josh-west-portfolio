Team Wear

Campaign Branding Social

Teamwear is a division of Adidas Football creating kits for amateur and semi-professional teams. The project involved rebranding Adidas Teamwear and showcasing new kit lines, with a goal of establishing a non-league visual identity that conveyed teamwork, the enjoyment of football, and mutual support. Imagery drawn from non-league grounds, locker rooms, and pitches was used throughout, with distorted headlines mimicking rippled shirt textures and distressed typography incorporating patterns from the shirts themselves.
