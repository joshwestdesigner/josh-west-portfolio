Adidas

Team Wear

Campaign Branding Social

Adidas Teamwear creates kits for amateur and semi-professional football clubs, the non-league world of Sunday sides, local rivalries and pitches that flood in October. Working as sole designer at IRIS Worldwide, the brief was to develop a visual identity for the division that felt true to that world rather than borrowed from Adidas's premium sportswear campaigns.

The concept drew directly from the texture of non-league football. Imagery came from dressing rooms, terraces and pitches rather than studios. Headlines were distorted to mimic the ripple of a shirt in motion, and typographic treatments incorporated patterns lifted from the kits themselves, connecting the design system back to the product.

Developed as a pitch to Adidas, the identity was built to celebrate football at its most honest: the team before the brand, the ground before the stadium.
