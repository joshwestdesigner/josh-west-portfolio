Starbucks

Phantom Frapp

Campaign Social Motion
Role
Designed, storyboarded and art directed the campaign animation
Studio
IRIS Worldwide
Goal
Launch a Halloween limited edition that feels spooky without losing the warmth of the Starbucks brand
Outcome
Campaign live across social and online editorial

Every Halloween, Starbucks launches a limited-edition drink with its own personality. Working as lead designer at IRIS Worldwide, the brief for the Phantom Frapp was to create something genuinely spooky without leaving the warmth of the Starbucks brand behind.

The concept treated the drink as a ghost of itself, present but fleeting and only here for the season. Visuals leaned into dark, atmospheric tones while keeping the brand's palette intact, balancing the eeriness with Starbucks's signature sense of playfulness. I handled the concept and retouching, then collaborated with an animator to bring the key visual to life for Starbucks's social channels. The campaign ran across social media and online editorial.
