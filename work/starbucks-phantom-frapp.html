Phantom Frapp

Campaign Social Motion

As part of the Starbucks halloween campaign, they wanted to advertise and release a new one off drink. This allowed me freedom to push the classic Starbucks brand to create something spooky and unique for the winter season.

The challenge was creating a personality for the drink that represented Halloween, felt current and didn't push the Starbucks brand too far. After securing approval on key visuals, I collaborated with an animator to produce storyboards for social media rollout.
