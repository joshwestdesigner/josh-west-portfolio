e for education

Campaign Branding OOH Social
Role
Lead Designer
Studio
Citi In-house Studio
Goal
Evolve the visual identity of a global education charity and extend it into campaign

e for education is a global charity initiative led by Citi, focused on improving access to education for children and young people around the world. Working as lead designer within Citi's in-house studio, the brief was to evolve the visual identity into something cleaner and more contemporary, capable of reflecting the programme's global scale.

Without a dedicated photoshoot, the imagery had to come from sourced material. I curated and art directed a photographic approach that could hold together across varied sources, selecting and retouching each image to unify tone, colour and light. Pulling out and enhancing greens across the set, and aligning key colour values, gave the campaign a consistency that felt deliberate rather than assembled.

In collaboration with a copywriter, we developed headlines designed to break across the composition, so that image and type completed each other rather than competing. That structural relationship made the retouching critical on two levels: consistency across the series, and ensuring each composition could naturally accommodate the split, with copy and image working as a single idea.

The campaign ran across the London Underground and social, giving the initiative a refreshed visual language with real presence at scale.
