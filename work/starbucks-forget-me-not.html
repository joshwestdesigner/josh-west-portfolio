Forget Me Not

Campaign Social OOH Branding
Role
Designed the key visual and extended it across social and in-store
Goal
Build a campaign world for a summer limited edition cup launch across social and in-store
Outcome
Campaign live across social and in-store
Studio
IRIS Worldwide

Forget-me-nots carry a ready-made emotional charge: small, vivid, and tied to the idea of being remembered. When Starbucks launched this summer limited edition with a beautifully illustrated reusable cup, that became the creative starting point.

Working as lead designer at IRIS Worldwide, I built the key visual outward from the cup's illustration, pulling its palette and botanical detail into a bold, layered composition that gave the drink its own distinct world. The campaign ran across social and in-store.
