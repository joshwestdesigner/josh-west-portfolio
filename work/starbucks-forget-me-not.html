Forget Me Not

Campaign Social OOH Branding

For this summer promotion, Starbucks launched a new limited-edition drink with a beautifully illustrated reusable cup. I created a key visual by drawing elements and colours directly from the cup's illustration to build a vibrant and colourful personality for the drink. The final visual was rolled out across social media and in-store promotional materials.
