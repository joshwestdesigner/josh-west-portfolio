Cinch

Eddie Stobart

Campaign Branding OOH

Cinch purchased ad space on the famous Eddie Stobart trucks. These trucks are famous for the names that are printed on them and are a perfect space to catch people's attention. Cinch recently rebranded and wanted to use this as an opportunity to push their new look and feel across the country. I worked closely with retouch and a copywriter to create a series of 3 designs to wrap the fleet, which then toured the country.
