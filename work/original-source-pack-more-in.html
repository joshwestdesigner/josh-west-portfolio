Pack More In

Campaign OOH Social
Role
Led design and art directed a 3D artist, retoucher and animator
Studio
IRIS Worldwide
Goal
Make a holiday competition feel like winning before you've even entered
Outcome
Campaign live in-store and online across the UK

Original Source ran a competition to win a holiday, and wanted a campaign that made you feel the possibility of winning before you'd even entered. Pack More In worked as both a product directive and a holiday promise.

The illustrated bottles were already in place when the project came to me — they became the creative springboard for everything that followed. I used them to develop the visual language and steer the wider art direction. To bring the key visual to life I art directed a team of specialists: a 3D artist and retoucher who built the final composite, and an illustrator who created the travel journal pages that thread through the frame. The campaign ran in-store and online.
