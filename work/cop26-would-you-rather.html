COP26

Would You Rather?

Campaign OOH Social
Role
Design & concept development
Studio
IRIS Worldwide
Goal
Create large-format OOH that made climate action feel immediate for COP26 delegates

COP26 brought world leaders to Glasgow with the weight of climate action on the agenda. Working at IRIS Worldwide alongside a senior art director, the brief was to develop a campaign that could meet them on their way in, using large-format outdoor advertising along the routes into the conference to land a message that was impossible to ignore.

The concept borrowed a game everyone already knows. Would You Rather? gave each execution a choice, pairing an absurd or uncomfortable option against a simple climate action and making the stakes feel immediate without resorting to doom. Illustrated political figures gave each piece its own absurdist character, while bold typography and a vivid palette ensured the work hit at scale. The pitch came second. The work was considered too brave.
