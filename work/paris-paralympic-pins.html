Citi

Paris Paralympic Pins

Branding Campaign

As a sponsor of the Paris Paralympic Games, Citi commissioned a series of collectible pins to be distributed across event venues and internally to employees.

The concept tapped into the established culture of pin trading at global sporting events, with a focus on creating a highly collectible system that would encourage exchange. I developed a modular design approach in which each pin represented a different country, while also forming part of a larger whole.

Each individual design was created to stand alone, but when brought together, the pins connected seamlessly to form a unified circular composition. This system added an additional layer of meaning and collectibility, rewarding audiences who engaged with the full set.

The final outcome balanced clarity at a small scale with a strong overarching visual idea, creating a memorable and cohesive series that aligned with the spirit of the Games.
