Citi

Paris Paralympic Pins

Branding Campaign
Role
Senior Designer
Studio
Citi In-house Studio
Goal
Turn a Paris 2024 sponsorship into a collectible gifting series for key stakeholders

Citi was a sponsor of the Paris 2024 Paralympic Games. This concept was developed within the in-house studio as a pitch for a series of collectible pins, one for each participating country, designed to be gifted as a complete set to key stakeholders.

Pin trading is a genuine tradition at events like this. The concept leaned into that culture by designing one pin per participating country, with every piece conceived as part of a larger system rather than a standalone keepsake.

Each pin works independently as a clean, considered piece of design. Together, they connect to form a single unified circular composition, giving the full set a reason to be displayed rather than just pocketed and adding a layer of meaning for whoever receives them.

Developed as a pitch concept, the series proposed a way to turn a sponsorship into something people would genuinely want to own.
