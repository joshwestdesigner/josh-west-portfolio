Heat RDY

Campaign Social OOH Motion

Heat RDY is Adidas's performance range built for athletes who push hardest in the heat. The concept was built around the technology in the clothing: thermal imaging, which makes heat visible as it moves through the body. The original plan was to shoot the athletes with a real thermal camera. The footage came back too low-resolution to use at campaign scale.

Rather than abandon the idea, I recreated the effect through creative retouching. Working with the retouching team at IRIS Worldwide, I developed a visual language that simulated thermal imaging across portraits of three athletes, dialling in the temperature palette and the way heat radiates through each frame. The concept survived. The work was just built a different way.

From the key visuals I developed social carousels for each athlete, then produced a design toolkit for Adidas's internal team to extend the campaign across markets.
