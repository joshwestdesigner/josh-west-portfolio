Adidas

Heat RDY

Campaign Social OOH Motion

This concept explored the intersection between technology and fashion. The photography of famous athletes was used alongside creative retouching in order to try and mimic the use of thermal cameras to create energy and movement in the imagery. I worked to create each key visual as well as social media carousels and finally a toolkit for internal Adidas designers to use for roll-outs.
